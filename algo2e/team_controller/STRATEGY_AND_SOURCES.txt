ALGO2e controller | Design and sources | 2026-10-01

One Python3.9+ process coordinates three strategies on one RIT account.
Standard library only. Default CLI operation is read-only. See START_HERE.txt.

MARKET MAKING
Quote CNR, RY and AC according to their signed fee schedules, inventory,
volatility, recent fill markouts and visible queue depth. Preserve unchanged
orders. Rank eligible quotes by a heuristic of edge, size and queue depth.
This ranking is not a calibrated fill-probability model.

TREND
Use confirmed fast/slow price changes, scaled by volatility, in case ticks.
Protective exits cancel stale exposure and actively reduce inventory opposing
the direction, rather than waiting indefinitely for passive fills. Balanced
also allows small directional targets and cost-gated active entries. Direction
can reverse; the strategy does not assume a repeated instructor price path.

PAIRS
Screen every stock pair using the preceding60 complete ticks, excluding the
current observation from regression. Require stable positive beta, positive
return correlation, mean-reverting residual behavior, repeated crossings and
an entry spread sufficient for conservative round-trip costs. Hedge quantities
by beta/dollar exposure. Reserve both legs before entry; unwind failed hedges,
broken relationships and timed-out spreads. No fundamental pair identity is
stated in the case, and the screen is not proof of cointegration. A full round
with no pair trades can be the correct outcome.

EXECUTION AND RISK
Use interval exposure bounds for every possible combination of outstanding
buy/sell fills. Aggregate share limits, per-asset share/notional caps and actual
venue limit bindings apply across strategies. Pair netting does not erase gross
exposure. Maintain cumulative fills and require matching positions before
another insertion. A cancel acknowledgement alone does not release capacity.
Unknown insertion outcomes never retry automatically. Monitor account loss,
drawdown, stale snapshots and long control-loop pauses. Stops cannot guarantee
an execution price or protect inventory while the computer is fully frozen.

Localhost aliases share a local writer lock. It cannot stop another computer
from trading the same account. One team operator is required. Automatic restart
into the next round is disabled; return to the menu and start deliberately.

RESEARCH SOURCES
1. Official ALGO2e Build1.00 brief:
https://github.com/RotmanFRTL/RotmanFRTL.github.io/blob/main/RIT%20-%20Case%20Brief%20-%20ALGO2e%20-%20Algorithmic%20Market%20Making%20Captone.pdf
The supplied copy matches its trading rules. 300ticks;5000/order;25000 aggregate
gross/net. CNR taker+.0027/maker+.0023; RY taker-.0014/maker-.0020;
AC taker+.0015/maker+.0011. Maker values are signed net credits. Marketable
limit portions take liquidity. Runtime metadata can change and must be checked.

2. Official Client REST documentation:
https://rit.306w.ca/RIT-REST-API/1.0.3/
Used for local Client authentication, order/status/cancel calls and metadata.
Global read/write budgets and security order-entry pacing are separate.

3. Official teaching starter:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RotmanFRTL/RotmanFRTL.github.io/main/ALGO2e%20base%20algo%20template.py
Useful endpoint reference; not adopted as the complete portfolio risk engine.
It does not reserve all possible pending fills, and its nonnegative gross
position is compared to a negative short threshold in the selling condition.

4. Public exact-case implementation:
https://github.com/YiMingChangJ/Algorithmic-Algo2e-Trading-Solution
Reviewed fee-aware quoting, inventory skew and queue preservation. Its per-stock
limit description differs from the supplied aggregate constraint, and generic
POST timeout retries can duplicate accepted orders without idempotency. Those
patterns were not adopted. External code was read, not executed or copied.

5. Directional market-making research:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4810
Fodra and Labadie model directional bets together with inventory constraints.
This motivates the architecture; it does not validate these parameters or P&L.

VALIDATION LIMITS
Tests exercise local mock transport, order races, fees, portfolio reservations,
trend reversals, admissible/rejected pairs and eight seeded synthetic rounds.
Synthetic fills are for correctness, not profit estimation. Historical market
snapshots cannot identify the passive queue fills of a strategy that was never
run. Parameters are not claimed optimal; trend signals can lose after costs.
Windows/class-server execution and profitable out-of-sample behavior require
practice on the actual simulator. No classroom orders are sent by self-tests.
